## Why the electrical titles (Search Console, 90 days to 2026-11-01)

The owner's Ahrefs screenshot showed position 1, 0 traffic, for three "what is an open ground" phrasings. Search
Console agrees on the traffic, not the position:
- The open-ground page averages position 10.7 over 2,416 impressions, with 4 clicks (0.17%).
- The three exact phrases had 7, 2 and 1 impressions, at average positions 12.0, 19.5 and 31.0.

Across the 30 most-shown pages, the same split holds:
- **Definition pages get few clicks:** open ground 0.17%, reverse polarity 0.35%.
- **Consequence pages get clicks:** fuse-box insurance 2.40% at position 5.3; splicing Romex to knob-and-tube 2.22%
  at 5.7; aluminum-wiring insurance 1.47%; knob-and-tube mortgage 1.11%; GFCI to pass inspection 1.06%.

The rule we're applying: write the "what happens to me" page (fix it, insure it, finance it) next to a defect we
already rank for. Google already shows the open-ground page for "open ground solutions" (position 25.7) and "what does
open ground mean on an outlet tester" (position 24.2), so that searcher has no page of ours yet.

Read the effect on the dates in each publish script. If the consequence pages don't beat about 1% click-through at
positions under 12 within 8 weeks, the pattern doesn't hold for new pages, so stop adding to it.

- **Taken:** the titles, the reader stage of each one, and the section ideas as a starting point.
- **Not taken without checking at the primary source:** every statistic and quote in its table. That includes the

  (check USGS/EPA directly), the "$10K–$20K" foundation range, and the Rocket Mortgage, AmeriMac and RE/MAX quotes.

  - The publishing schedule of one a month.
