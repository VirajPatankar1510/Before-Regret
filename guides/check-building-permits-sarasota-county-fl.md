# Sarasota County Permit Search: Accela Login & AMANDA Records

Sarasota County keeps its building permits in the [Accela Civic Application](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SARASOTACO/Default.aspx), and the first thing most people hit there is a login page. The county's own FAQ answers the question plainly: "To search records/permits in Accela, you must have an account." Registration is free, but before you make one, check that the county is even the right office for the address. In much of what people call Sarasota, it isn't.

## Which office holds the permits for your address

A Sarasota mailing address says nothing about who issued the permits. The county's Building Division handles the unincorporated areas, while the City of Sarasota, Venice and North Port each run their own building office and their own search.

| If the house is in | Permits are kept by | Where to search |
|---|---|---|
| Unincorporated Sarasota County | Sarasota County Building Division, 941-861-5000, building@scgov.net | [Accela, agency SARASOTACO](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SARASOTACO/Default.aspx) (login needed) |
| City of Sarasota | City Building & Permitting, 1575 2nd Street, (941) 263-6494 | The Building Permit Lookup linked from the city's [Building & Permitting page](https://www.sarasotafl.gov/Department-Pages/Development-Services/Building-Permitting) |
| City of Venice | Venice Building and Code Enforcement, (941) 882-7547 | The city's [online permitting service](https://www.venicegov.com/government/building-and-code) |
| City of North Port | North Port Permitting | The city's [Permit Lookup](https://www.northportfl.gov/Building-Planning/Permitting/Permit-Lookup), a separate Accela agency (NORTHPORT) |
| Town of Longboat Key | Ask the town | We couldn't open the town's website to confirm |

The quickest check is the Sarasota County Property Appraiser's [property search](https://www.sc-pa.com/propertysearch), which shows the parcel's location and ID. That ID also works in the county's Accela search, which lists PID among its search options.

The split covers a lot of homes. We ran the Census Bureau's year-built table (American Community Survey, 2020–2024) for the county and took out the three cities. Of 266,492 housing units in Sarasota County, 174,272, or 65.4%, are outside the City of Sarasota, Venice and North Port. Of those, 6,248 are in the Sarasota County part of Longboat Key, whose permit office we couldn't confirm. Everything else outside the three cities is the county's.

## Searching the county's Accela portal

Sarasota County's Accela portal is organized by module: Building, Community Development, Licenses, Public Works, Enforcement and Fire. Building permits for houses sit in the Building module. When we opened that search on November 28, 2026 without an account, it sent us straight to the login page with the message "This feature requires registration and/or login, please login to continue."

Two county pages disagree about this. The Online Permitting page marks Public Works, Enforcement and General Information Request as showing nothing without a login, and doesn't mark Building. The FAQ says any records search needs an account. The portal behaved the way the FAQ says, so plan on registering.

Once you're signed in, the FAQ's General Search takes a record or permit number, a record type, a contractor's license number or name, an address or a PID. You can download the results list, which is handy when an older house has a long history.

## Permits closed before August 18, 2023

Sarasota County's FAQ sends anyone "Looking for Permits Closed Prior to August 18, 2023?" to the older AMANDA system. The Accela home page links that archive at building.scgov.net. The cut-off is the date a permit was closed, not the date it was issued, so an older permit that was finaled before then sits in AMANDA, while one that was still open on that date should show in Accela.

Most houses will have records on both sides. Our Census figures put 59% of the homes outside the three cities as built before 1990 and only 3% as built in 2020 or later, so for almost every house the first construction permit was closed long before 2023. Look in AMANDA for a house's first permits and older jobs, and in Accela for anything recent. We couldn't load building.scgov.net from here on three tries, and AMANDA's starting year is still unknown to us.

## Work that legally has no permit

Since July 1, 2026, Florida Statutes § 553.79(1)(g) has made local governments exempt single-family owners from a permit for "any work valued at less than $7,500". The county's Building page points to its Bulletin 2026-0002 for how to qualify. The exemption doesn't apply in a flood hazard area, or to electrical, plumbing, structural, mechanical or gas work.

A recent repair with no permit in Accela may therefore be legal. The statute requires a written exemption request with the contract to go to the building department, so ask the seller for a copy of that request, or ask the county whether one was filed for the address.

## No account, or the search shows nothing

Public records don't depend on an Accela account. Email building@scgov.net or call 941-861-5000 with the address and the parcel ID, and ask for the permit history. In person, the county's Building staff are at the PDS One Stop, 870 Apex Road in Sarasota, and at the R.L. Anderson Administration Center, 4000 S. Tamiami Trail in Venice.

For a formal check of liens on a property, the county FAQ says you have to create an application for a General Information Request, and that module needs a login too.

An open permit from a previous owner is worth sorting out before closing, but Florida law protects a buyer from being fined for it. Under § 553.79(17)(a), a building department may not penalize an arm's-length buyer solely because a previous owner's permit wasn't closed. The steps for closing out an old permit are in [this Florida open-permit walkthrough](/guides/resolve-open-building-permits-before-closing/).
